The Gita begins with someone who wants to walk away.

Between two armies, Arjuna puts down his bow and says he will not fight. Krishna's answer to that one frozen moment is the whole Gita.

क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्यक्त्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप ॥

“Shake off this base faint-heartedness and arise, O conqueror of foes!”

BG 2.3, translated by Swami Adidevananda
